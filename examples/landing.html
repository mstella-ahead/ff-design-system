Why measurement infrastructure decides yield

As devices stack into compact multi-die packages, the cost of finding a defect late rises with every step it survives. Test infrastructure is the shortest lever on that cost.

The same probe technology that qualifies a process in engineering has to survive millions of touchdowns in production — which makes contact repeatability, not raw throughput, the number worth optimising.
